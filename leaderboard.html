The competition runs 6–7 October 2026. The first standings appear here on the evening of Day 1, after Round 1 is evaluated.

How standings are produced

  1. The round closes and all submissions lock on the server clock.
  2. Objective components are scored automatically — checkable findings, and model performance against private test data.
  3. Judges review where human assessment applies.
  4. Scores are finalised and qualification decided.
  5. The standings are published here, and announced in the WhatsApp group.

Only rank, team, score and qualification status are shown. Component breakdowns and challenge assignments stay private.
